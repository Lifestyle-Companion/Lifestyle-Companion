HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.18
14 August 2026

FOCUS: DD-like guided food entry with HEC Australian intelligence and safer data integrity.

• Progress History now offers 7 Days, 2 Weeks, 30 Days, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year and All.
• Removed the Food Diary Days / Weight Check-Ins / Activities summary box.
• Fish-finger results that only have gram data now offer a clearly-labelled estimated per-finger unit as well as grams.
• Copy Food can copy to the same day or another day and explicitly choose Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snacks/Other.
• Recent keeps the current meal filter, allows row-tap quantity/unit review, and keeps deliberate quick repeats while blocking accidental rapid double-taps.
• Search Back restores the prior query/tab/scroll state consistently.
• Sausage gets a DD-style guided path: meat → plain/flavoured → cooking method → size/grams. Search words already supplied skip redundant questions.
• Australian chain discovery added for KFC Australia, McDonald’s Australia/Macca’s, and Hungry Jack’s.
• Burger King is suppressed as an Australian chain search and points users to Hungry Jack’s.
• Chain menu discovery never turns missing nutrition into 0 Cal. A small set of current official Australian items is included as verified Alpha 0.6.18 snapshots; unverified menu items are discoverable but blocked from Diary entry until current nutrition is verified.
• Existing Alpha 0.6 browser-storage keys and personal libraries are retained.

DATA NOTE: restaurant menus change. Alpha 0.6.18 deliberately separates menu discovery from nutrition authority so a later protected shared-data updater can refresh menus and values without changing the food-entry workflow.
