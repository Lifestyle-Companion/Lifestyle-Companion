HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.8

Focus: stabilise onboarding, food entry, Diary use, hydration, voice logging and weight history before barcode and nutrition-panel scanning.

Key changes:
- Fresh-start testing remains available from Settings > Data, Backup & Privacy > Reset App for Testing.
- Australian state/territory is selected from a list after choosing Australia.
- Companion cards open an immediate detail preview instead of requiring a long scroll.
- Stronger heading hierarchy and consistent Title Case for major headings.
- Detailed Nutrition Goals are collapsed by default; Daily Fluids remains visible.
- Ordinary Diary entries count immediately. Planned/Eaten confirmation is removed from normal use.
- Clear meal and Delete Food controls are available.
- Food search is adjusted for the mobile keyboard.
- Voice logging requires a meal selection when no meal context is known.
- Quick Add Drink asks what was consumed and sends nutrient drinks to the Diary.
- Weight check-ins prevent unchanged duplicate saves and use brief spoken confirmation.
- Weight Trend uses readable Australian dates and tighter scaling.
- Familiar Australian generic foods and meals were added to improve testing coverage.

Barcode and nutrition-panel capture remain the next major development stage after this stabilisation round.
